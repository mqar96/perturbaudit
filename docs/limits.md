# Limits

- True but weak effects below the screen's power are called unsupported.
- Knockdown (total) effects cannot prove direct regulation; direct effects that cancel within total effects are missed.
- Decoy-based false-discovery estimates bound only the noise part of the error; systematic artefacts need the other checks.
- The guide-specific check alone catches about two thirds of planted off-target artefacts in simulation; seed maps catch 93–100%.
- Transfer to other cell lines is a strong test only with platform-matched data: K562 effects replicate far less in an
  independent lab's HCT116 screen than in same-lab RPE1, HepG2 and Jurkat screens.
