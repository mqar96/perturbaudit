The code in this repository is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). The result tables and figures are data, not code:

- Tables in `results/` are derived from public CC BY 4.0 screens (Replogle et al. 2022; Nadig et al. 2025; Adamson et al.
  2016; Norman et al. 2019; Tian et al. 2021; Bradu et al. 2026; Zhu et al. 2026) and simulations and public annotations, and are shared under
  CC BY 4.0 with attribution to those sources (see `DATA.md`). No table here contains X-Atlas/Orion (HCT116) data.
- Results derived from the Xaira Therapeutics X-Atlas/Orion HCT116 screen (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) are distributed only in the
  separate CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 Zenodo record (see `CITATION.cff`), with attribution to Xaira Therapeutics.
- Figures that contain HCT116-derived panels (`fig_m4_genomewide_seed.png`, `Fig4_transfer_matched.png`,
  `fig_cross_platform.png`, `fig_concordance_4lines.png`) are shared under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; all other figures under CC BY 4.0.
